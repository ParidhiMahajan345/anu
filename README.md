AnubhavX
Discover Jammu Beyond the Pilgrimage

AnubhavX is an immersive tourism and cultural exploration platform designed to make Jammu’s lesser-known heritage sites, cultural spaces, local markets, and spiritual destinations more discoverable through immersive technology.

The platform combines Virtual Reality, 3D environments, mobile technology, and interactive experiences to help users explore Jammu’s cultural identity beyond the conventional pilgrimage route.

🌏 The Problem

Jammu is widely known for its pilgrimage tourism, but many of its forts, palaces, temples, museums, heritage sites, traditional markets, and cultural spaces remain outside the typical tourist journey.

Visitors may also face limitations related to:

Distance and travel time
Accessibility
Lack of awareness about lesser-known destinations
Limited digital representation of cultural assets
Difficulty experiencing multiple destinations within a short trip

This creates a discoverability gap between Jammu’s cultural assets and potential visitors.

💡 Our Solution

AnubhavX bridges this gap by digitally recreating heritage and cultural environments as immersive experiences.

The platform enables users to:

Discover lesser-known destinations
Explore immersive 3D environments
Experience heritage and cultural spaces virtually
Interact with elements within virtual environments
Explore local markets and cultural spaces
Learn about destinations before planning physical visits
Access experiences through VR, mobile applications, and VR kiosks
✨ Key Features
🏛️ Immersive Heritage Exploration

Digitally recreated heritage environments allow users to explore important cultural locations virtually.

🛍️ Local Market & Cultural Experiences

AnubhavX aims to digitally represent traditional markets and cultural spaces, making them more discoverable.

🥽 Virtual Reality

Users can experience immersive environments through VR headsets.

📱 Mobile Application

The platform extends accessibility through a mobile application.

🖥️ VR Kiosks

The experience can be deployed through VR kiosks at suitable tourism, exhibition, and public locations.

🎮 Interactive Experiences

Users can interact with objects and elements within the virtual environment.

🎧 Immersive Audio

Future field-ready versions will incorporate real-location audio and spatial sound to enhance realism.

🎮 Current Prototype

The initial AnubhavX prototype was developed using Unity 3D as a basic immersive simulation.

The prototype demonstrates:

VR-based movement
3D temple environment
Interactive bell
Interactive offerings
VR controller interaction
Voice-based prompts
Smart hardware experimentation

The project is now being developed toward more realistic, location-based immersive experiences using real-world heritage and cultural content.
